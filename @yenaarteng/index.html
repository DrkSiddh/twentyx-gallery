THE TWENTYX ARTIST HANDBOOK

Twenty pieces. One point of view.

The best exhibitions aren't just twenty uploads. They're twenty reasons to keep looking.

Curate an encounter, not a dump.

01 /
A visual autobiography

Begin with your earliest artistic obsession. End with who you've become. Let each image earn its place.

02 /
Twenty variations, one idea

One subject, twenty interpretations: a skull, a color, a city block, a memory. Give repetition a purpose.

03 /
The curator's mixtape

Sequence as you would an album: an opening track, a surprise, a quiet interlude, a finale.

04 /
Past / present / possible

Five origins, ten experiments, five future visions. Use contrast to tell an unfolding story.

05 /
The secret exhibition

Give viewers clues—recurring symbols, hidden relationships, a punchline waiting at artwork twenty.

Keep the originals intact. Let framing, order, and context do the storytelling.

TwentyX, without the guesswork.

How do I submit?

Join the ArtVision community on X. Publish one original X post with exactly twenty images, and include #TwentyX. The exhibition discovers qualifying public posts when the information is available.

Why exactly twenty images?

TwentyX is a deliberately constrained format: twenty works, curated by their artist, within one post. A partial media preview does not establish eligibility.

Will my originals be modified?

TwentyX displays your publicly shared images as an exhibition. The interface and brochure layout may change, but the original artworks are not AI-generated or recolored by the gallery.

When will I appear?

Publication windows are 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM Pacific. Discovery depends on access to X's public post information, so inclusion cannot be guaranteed at a particular time.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

No. TwentyX participation is an art exhibition experience, not a wallet-gated application.

Can visitors follow me?

Yes. They can explore your twenty works on TwentyX and choose to follow you on X in a separate window.

Participation terms

TwentyX by ArtVision · Participation guidelines

1. Original work and permissions

Share only images you created or have permission to exhibit. You remain responsible for the rights and accuracy of your submission.

2. Exhibition display

By intentionally submitting a public post with #TwentyX, you request consideration for display within TwentyX. If included, your publicly posted works and X attribution may be displayed in the gallery, featured in a rotating spotlight, and reproduced in a free exhibition brochure. You retain ownership of your artwork.

3. Curation and eligibility

A submission must contain twenty distinct images in one original post, with #TwentyX. ArtVision may decline or remove works that fail eligibility, infringe rights, or violate applicable platform rules. Submission does not guarantee acceptance or a feature slot.

4. Removal requests

Contact ArtVision through the X community with your original post URL to request a review or removal of your exhibition.

5. Third-party platform

Following artists or joining the X community is performed on X and subject to X's own terms. TwentyX does not act on your X account on your behalf.

These participation guidelines describe this exhibition; they do not override your copyright or X's platform terms.
